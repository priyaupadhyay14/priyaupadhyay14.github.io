    <h3 id="meal-delivery">🚚 Meal Delivery Optimization Model (MDOM)</h3>
    <p>Built an optimization model in Excel to solve a real logistics problem — assigning couriers to delivery orders in a way that minimizes total delivery time while keeping workloads balanced.
